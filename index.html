Why does this count as a T-spin?

A T-spin requires a rotation as the final positioning action, with at least three of the four diagonal corners around the T's center occupied by blocks or boundaries. Orange markers identify those three corners here. After the rotation, both corners facing the T's tip are occupied, so this is a full T-spin. Completing two rows makes it a T-spin Double.

Soft drop (↓) leaves the piece movable. Hard drop (Space) locks it immediately. Rotate into the slot first, then hard drop. In this setup the hard drop travels zero cells, preserving the rotation for spin detection.
